How this works

  • You can hold exactly one bank account. Once it is linked this form goes away for good.
  • The number has to belong to an account that already exists and nobody has claimed. A number someone else holds, or one that does not exist, is refused.
  • Money moves either way, and is always limited to what the account it is leaving actually holds.
  • Each transfer carries its own reference, so pressing the button twice will not pay you twice.
